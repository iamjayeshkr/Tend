TEND · PRIVACY & PERMISSIONS

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 · Version 1.1
Our Core Privacy Pledge: Tend is private by design. No account. No cloud analytics. No third-party advertisements. Your entries, tasks, and usage metrics never leave your device.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

All data generated within Tend is stored locally on your device (using IndexedDB and Android SharedPreferences):

  • Personal commitments, tasks, and completion statuses.
  • Focus session logs, durations, and reflection notes.
  • Custom daily rhythms, routines, and wake/sleep times.
  • App restriction settings and daily minute limits.

We do not operate servers that collect, store, or process your personal data.

2. Device Permissions & Why We Request Them

  • Accessibility Service (Optional): Used strictly to detect when a user-selected restricted app (e.g. Instagram or YouTube) enters the foreground, enabling Tend to show the gentle focus lock screen. Tend never accesses on-screen text, chats, passwords, keystrokes, or personal data.
  • Usage Access (Optional): Used solely to calculate daily screen time for restricted apps against your chosen minute budget.
  • Activity Recognition (Optional): Used locally to detect morning wakefulness (via step counting and motion sensors) to protect your morning focus without relying on naive wall-clock timers.
  • Exact Alarms & Notifications: Used solely to trigger your local wake-up, wind-down, and routine reminders on time.

3. Google Play Protect & Accessibility Compliance

AccessibilityService API Usage Disclosure:

Tend declares and uses an Android AccessibilityService (app.tend.ProtectionService) strictly for digital wellbeing and focus protection.

  • What Tend does: Listens for typeWindowStateChanged events to observe foreground package names of user-selected apps to enforce focus blocks and time limits.
  • What Tend NEVER does: Tend does NOT read on-screen content (canRetrieveWindowContent="false"), does NOT record keystrokes (canRequestFilterKeyEvents="false"), does NOT perform automated gestures (canPerformGestures="false"), and does NOT transmit data over the network.

4. No Third-Party Trackers or Advertising

Tend contains zero third-party analytics libraries (no Google Firebase, Facebook SDK, or telemetry trackers) and zero advertising frameworks. The application works completely offline.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can export a JSON backup at any time from You > Preferences & backups. To permanently erase all data, simply clear app storage in Android Settings or uninstall Tend.
